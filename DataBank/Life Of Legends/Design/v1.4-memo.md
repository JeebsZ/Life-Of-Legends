# v1.4 memo — 5 Nov 2026

**Built by subagents, reviewed by me.** **293 tests pass, lint clean, APK builds** (arm64 86 MB, 5 Nov 00:28). Getting it to build needed two fixes: a Dart int type error in the vision add-on, and a Kotlin version check that `pyproject.toml` now sets to warn instead of fail, because of tflite_flutter.
- **Voice cut-off fixed (cause found):**
  - On Android every sound effect took exclusive audio focus, which stopped Lumen mid-line.
  - Now all players mix together, set at app start in our own extension.
  - Side effect: app sounds now play over your music instead of pausing it.
- **Sci-fi pack mapped:** 17 events now use pack sounds, and 4 of them are new (distort, scan tick/done, camera shutter). Only the 20 files used ship (1.3 MB). level_up, milestone, skill_acquired and lumen_hum keep the synth versions because they're bassier. Mapping table: `tools/sfx/README.md`.
- **Effects:**
  - **Icosphere:** an animated wireframe icosphere at the heart of Quest Cleared, Notice, Milestone, Identify and Path level-up.
  - **Screen distortion:** zoom punch, shake, colour-split ghost, glitch slices, scanline sweep and a shockwave.
- **Staged analysis everywhere Lumen "thinks":** Report, Insights, starting a chain and generating steps, skill rank-up, "Give me a quest", backup restore, Body plan and meal scan. Quick taps stay instant.
- **Body (More → Track):**
  - Weight log with trend and BMI (with its limits noted).
  - A goal turns into a daily kcal range plus protein, carbs and fat, with a safety floor.
- **Meals:**
  - Daily log with 214 foods, Filipino dishes included.
  - **Camera scan** runs on the phone itself, free and offline, recognising about 2,000 dishes.
  - A confirm card shows each item as a range; your scale grams override the estimate.

# Achievements — planning list (v1.2, no code yet)

Status: written list only, per v1.2 spec C.7. Visual badge design comes later.

## Rules for every achievement
- Earned by **effort and exploration**: trying things, showing up, coming back, finishing.
- **Never** based on body weight or shape, food amounts, money spent or saved, investment results, or perfect streaks.
- Nothing can be lost. A badge stays earned even after a break, a deleted quest or an archived bill.
- Each badge shows its progress ("3 / 5 Paths tried") and a one-line "how to earn it".
- Tiers are optional: **Bronze → Silver → Gold** (same badge, bigger frame). Tier numbers below are first guesses.
- Scope: **Account** (whole profile) or **Path** (one badge per Path, tinted with that Path's color).
- All data needed already exists in the save: quests `done_on`, side quest steps, skills, logs, focus sessions, bill payments, templates, redemptions.

## Not doing (on purpose)
- No "lose X kg", calorie, step-count or body goals.
- No "spend X coins" or "save X pesos" badges (spending coins is fine, it is just not an achievement target).
- No "30 days in a row" or "never miss" badges; no badge that resets.
- No badges for paying bills on time: a late bill must never feel like a failure in the game.

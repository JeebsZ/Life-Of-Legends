# v1.2 spec — "Arcane System" look, SFX, Phase-1 features

Sources:
- `Design/expanded-feature-design.md`, the user's feature doc.
- `Design/refs/1-6`, the user's mood images.
- Earlier specs: v1, v1.1, lumen-voice, ui-system.

**IP rule:** the references are mood only. Build original art: no kanji or names taken from the references, no show logos, no copied frames. All labels are in English, or use original made-up glyphs.

## A. Look and effects (map each reference to a moment)
| Ref | Moment | Original effect to build |
|---|---|---|
| 1 notice | **Skill / notice acquired** | Diamond (rotated-square) emblem, thin double frame, title stacked inside. A light burst from the top: radial gradient that flares then fades, plus a starfield of drifting particles. Cyan/teal glow. |
| 2 milestone | **Overall level-up, rank-up, achievement** | Concentric rotating gold rings of original rune glyphs. Inner square emblem with circuit lines. Bright core flare, light rays. Lasts about 2.5 s, tap to skip. |
| 3 Lumen thinking | **Lumen "Analysis…" (Ask Lumen, weekly report, onboarding greeting)** | Wireframe polyhedron/orb rotating around a bright core, with flying square particles and radial lines. Plays while the proposal "computes" (about 0.8 s). |
| 4 identifying | **Unlock / identify** (theme bought, side quest started, item obtained) | Gear-ring and clock-face texture rotating behind big bold title text. A chain wraps across and snaps open on reveal. |
| 5 skill links | **Skill tree / linked quests** | Framed skill nodes (rectangular, thin borders, a small center tag such as "ACQUIRED") joined by lines. Used in Me → Skills and the side-quest chain view. Show the next suggested node. |
| 6 timed | **Focus timer and anything timed** | Spiral of concentric clock rings with numerals, slowly rotating, bright center. The timer's progress ring sits on top. |

**Rules:**
- Effects are fast, never block input for more than about 2.5 s, and can be skipped by tapping.
- Keep a "Reduce effects" toggle in settings: off = full effects, on = simple fades.
- All of it is built with Flet built-ins: Canvas, gradients, rotate/scale/opacity animations. No new dependencies unless the user OKs them.
- Arcane stays the default theme. Keep the existing juice (XP bar, pop, floaters, Lumen notice).

## B. Sound (SFX on top of the voicepack)
- The user likes Opera GX's tab open/close sounds. Those are copyrighted, so we **synthesize original sounds in a similar airy, techy style**. Never sample them.
- Generator: `tools/sfx/make_sfx.py`, pure Python stdlib, no installs. It writes WAV files to `src/assets/sfx/`.
- **Sound set:**
  - **Navigation:** `tab_open` (soft rising airy sweep + glassy click), `tab_close` (falling version), `tap`, `toggle`, `sheet_open`, `sheet_close`.
  - **Rewards and moments:** `quest_done` (bright chime), `coin`, `xp_fill` (soft shimmer), `level_up` (rising arpeggio + shimmer), `skill_acquired` (deep impact + sparkle), `milestone` (bigger version).
  - **Notices and alerts:** `notice` (ping before Lumen speaks), `alert_soft` (bill due), `error_soft`.
  - **Timer:** `timer_tick` (optional, very soft), `timer_done` (gentle bell).
- `audio.py` gets `sfx(name)` alongside the voice clips. SFX can overlap; voice clips don't.
- Settings: SFX on/off and volume, separate from voice. Respect "Reduce effects" (quieter).

## C. Phase-1 features (from the feature doc). Phases 2-4 come later.
1. **Edit** quests, rewards and bills.
   - **Quests:** change title, Path, difficulty, kind, note. Keep completion history.
   - **Rewards:** change name and price. Redemption history stays.
   - **Bills:** change name, amount, due date and repeat. Payment history stays. Archive is separate from delete.
   - **Coin limit on custom tasks:** custom quests can't set coins above the cap for their difficulty.
2. **Quest templates:** a starter library that includes self-care (skin care, oral care, facial, stretching), gym, weekly planning and meal prep. Templates are editable; save any quest as a template; create a quest from a template.
3. **Chains, extended:** side quests gain **ordered vs any-order** steps, a target date, pause/skip/reorder/edit steps, and "generate a learning chain" for any skill name (template-based generator, no AI). Show the next step on Today.
4. **Focus timer:**
   - Modes: focus/break intervals (custom lengths), stopwatch, pause/resume.
   - Optionally link it to a quest or Path.
   - On completion, log the session. Partial sessions count, and the session earns Path XP (with a daily cap).
5. **Calendar:** month and week views (day view is the list). Shows quests done, side-quest steps, logs and bill due dates, with filter chips. Blank days are neutral, with no "broken streak" styling.
6. **Backup and export:** full JSON backup and restore (with a confirm step) plus CSV export of tables. Uses `FilePicker.save_file`, or the share sheet on mobile. Show what's included.
7. **Achievements:** do **last**. Only a written list for now (`Design/achievements.md`), effort and exploration based. Visual badge design comes later.

## D. Navigation
The bottom bar stays at 5 buttons: Today · Quests · Bills · Rewards · **More**.

**More** opens a grid of icon tiles, similar to how GCash shows its services:
- Profile (Me), Skills, Calendar, Focus Timer, Templates, Logbook, Lumen Report, Backup & Export, Settings (voice, SFX, effects, theme), and Achievements (coming soon).

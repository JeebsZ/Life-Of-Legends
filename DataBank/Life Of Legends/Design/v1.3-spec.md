# v1.3 spec: adjustments + Phase 2

User answers, 4 Nov, after testing v1.2 on the phone.

## Decisions
- **Navigation:** keep the 5 tabs with **More** last. Everything extra, including Me/Profile, lives in More. Already done.
- **Chains:** follow the feature doc: steps run in order **or** all available, as a per-chain toggle. Already done.
- **Editing is locked down after creation.**
  - **Custom quest:** name, note and difficulty only. No changing Path, kind, XP or coins after creation.
  - **Reward:** name and note only. The price is fixed once created.
  - **Bill:** name, note and **paid/unpaid** only. Amount, due date and repeat are fixed.
  - The core keeps its fuller edit functions internally, but the UI and public flow expose only the above.
- **Self-care:** a new 7th Path, **Monk** (self-care, hygiene, calm routines). The name can be changed later. Self-care templates move to it.
- **Name:** Life of Legends.

## Fixes from the phone test
1. **Voice and sound overlap** when a quest is completed. Sound effects must **duck or finish before** Lumen's voice: play the chime, then the voice about 0.15 s after the chime's audible part ends. The coin and XP sounds stay soft and short.
2. **No effect on quest completion.** Add a short **QuestCleared** effect, about 1.1 s and tap-through: a small diamond flash, flare and sparks with "QUEST CLEARED". It shows on every completion. A level-up or skill unlock still plays its big effect instead.
3. **Lumen intro:** every LumenThinking starts with a heavy cinematic **hum**. It's original: a deep drone and sub-bass swell, a soft whoosh, then a crystalline shimmer as the orb appears (about 1.5–2 s). Add a `lumen_hum` SFX.
4. **Slower analysis:** LumenThinking runs about 2.2–2.8 s for Ask Lumen and generating steps (with a slower scan bar and staged text: "ANALYSIS…" → "MATCHING PATHS…" → "PROPOSAL READY"). Tap to skip still works.
5. **More body in the sounds:** add low-end hum/bass layers to the reward sounds (quest_done, level_up, skill_acquired, milestone, notice). Navigation clicks stay light.

## Content
Many more Quest Board quests and side-quest chains for Lumen to suggest:
- At least 8 board quests per Path.
- At least 4 chains per Path (each with rank 2 and 3 follow-ups).
- All 7 Paths.

Where it fits, reference **free, legal** learning resources: Project Gutenberg / Standard Ebooks public-domain books, OpenStax textbooks, MIT OpenCourseWare, Khan Academy, freeCodeCamp, the Internet Archive's public-domain titles, and government health/finance guides. Store a title + URL in the step note. Sources are listed in `Design/content-sources.md`.

## Notifications (user wants them)
- **Approach:** local notifications scheduled on the device. Works offline and needs no server.
- **Engine:** a **Flet extension we write ourselves** that wraps the widely used Flutter plugin `flutter_local_notifications`, plus the official `flet-permission-handler` for the Android 13+ notification permission. Avoid unvetted third-party Python wrappers.
- **Uses:** bill due reminders (X days before, at a set time), daily quest reminder, water reminders (interval, quiet hours, active days, snooze), focus timer finished, gentle comeback nudge (off by default).
- **Settings:** a master toggle, per-category toggles, quiet hours.
- **Fallback alternative:** export reminders to the device calendar (.ics).

## Phase 2 (from expanded-feature-design.md §4, 14, 18–20)
- **Workouts:**
  - Sessions with type, duration, effort and notes, linked to a quest or Path.
  - Strength exercises with sets, reps and weight (kg/lb).
  - Routines/templates, copy a past session, per-exercise history and personal records (descriptive only), rest timer.
- **Sleep:**
  - Bedtime and wake time, which can cross midnight.
  - Naps and a restedness rating (1–5).
  - Edit later, weekly average, simple trend chart.
- **Water:**
  - Daily goal set by the user (no universal target), quick-add buttons, custom amounts, units.
  - History and progress.
  - Reminders (see Notifications).
  - Small XP for reaching the daily goal, capped.
- **Energy check-in:** low / okay / high plus an optional note. It sorts suggestions, never blocks anything, and can be turned off.
- **Insights:**
  - Plain, ranged trends: quests by Path, workouts per week, sleep duration, water.
  - Show the data behind each one. No claims that one thing causes another, and no health advice.
- **Navigation:** More gets tiles for Workout, Sleep, Water, Energy and Insights. Today gets a water quick-add and an energy chip if they're enabled. Modules can be turned on or off in Settings.

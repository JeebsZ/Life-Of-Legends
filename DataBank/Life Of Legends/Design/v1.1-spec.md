# v1.1 spec: Voice, Guest onboarding, Side quests

Decisions (user, 4 Nov):
- **Voice:** a voicepack of clips, calm female, made with Piper on the PC.
- **Login:** guest sign-in now, with name and avatar. Google sign-in comes later, together with cloud backup.
- **Side quests:** preset skill chains, make-your-own, and skill ranks.

## 1. Side quests and skills (core: `src/lol/core`)
- **SideQuest** (a chain) = `id, skill_name, path_id, steps: list[Step], custom: bool, rank_target: int (1-3), created`. **Step** = `title, note, xp, coins, done_on: str|None`.
- Completing a step gives its XP and coins, uses the normal daily coin cap, and is idempotent. Completing the **last step unlocks the skill** at its rank.
- **Skill** = `name, path_id, rank: 1 Novice | 2 Apprentice | 3 Adept, unlocked_on, chain_ids`.
  - Rank 1 unlocks from a chain.
  - Ranks 2 and 3 come from "follow-up" chains for the same skill. The player starts these when they choose to; they're never assigned automatically.
  - XP bonus for unlocking or ranking up: about 2× a step. Balance constants go at the top of `game.py`.
- **Catalog:** 12 or more preset chains, at least 2 per Path, 3 steps each, plus one follow-up per skill. Example: *Marketing* (Scholar): read a marketing book (or 3 chapters) → write 3 takeaways → try one idea on something real. Other ideas: Budgeting (Steward), Knife Skills (Chef), Bodyweight Basics (Warrior), Sketching (Artisan), Trail Walking (Ranger), Public Speaking, Photography, Basic Coding. Wording stays safe and beginner-level.
- **Custom chain:** `add_side_quest(skill_name, path_id, steps: list[str])` with 1–5 steps, default XP and coins per step by difficulty.
- **API (on `Game`):**
  - `side_quests(active_only=False)`, `start_side_quest(catalog_id, today) -> SideQuest`, `add_side_quest(...)`
  - `complete_step(sq_id, step_index, today) -> Completion` (with extra fields `skill_unlocked: str|None`, `skill_rank: int|None`)
  - `skills() -> list[Skill]`, `followups_for(skill_name)`, `abandon_side_quest(sq_id)` (keeps XP already earned).
  - The existing `Game.skills()` (Quest Board titles) gets replaced or merged. Board quests can stay as "practices", but the Skills list now means unlocked skills.
- **Profile:** add `avatar_id: str` (default "a1"), `onboarded: bool` (default False for new saves, **True for migrated or existing saves**), `voice_on: bool = True`, `voice_volume: float = 0.8`.
- **Save:** bump to `"schema": 2` with migration from 1, and keep v0 → 2 working. No data loss. Tests cover all of this.

## 2. Voicepack (UI and assets)
- New dependency: `flet-audio` 1.0.5. Piper runs on the PC only, in `tools/voice/.venv`, and is **not** in the app's dependencies.
- Clips in `src/assets/voice/<kind>_<n>.ogg` (or `.wav` if ogg isn't possible), 2–3 variants per kind. Lines are **generic, with no numbers**:
  - "Notice. Quest cleared."
  - "Confirmed. Level up."
  - "Confirmed. Path level increased."
  - "Skill acquired."
  - "Proposal ready."
  - "Advisory. A bill is due soon."
  - "Confirmed. Payment recorded."
  - "Reward granted."
  - "Today's coin limit reached."
  - "Welcome back."
  - "Analysis complete."
  - a first-launch greeting: "Hello. I am Lumen. I will guide your progress."
- `tools/voice/lines.json` maps clip names to text, and `tools/voice/make_voice.py` regenerates every clip.
- `src/lol/ui/audio.py`: `play(kind)` picks the variant that matches the on-screen line. It respects `profile.voice_on` and volume, never blocks the UI, and does nothing on its own if audio fails.
- LumenNotice plays the clip when a notice starts.

## 3. Guest onboarding (UI)
- On first launch (`onboarded == False`) show a short flow before the tabs:
  1. Lumen greeting, typed out with voice.
  2. Your name.
  3. Pick an avatar (8 or more icon/color avatars, original and no IP).
  4. Pick your first Path, which gives a starter side quest suggestion.
- Buttons: **"Continue as guest"** (primary) and a muted **"Sign in with Google — coming with cloud backup"** (disabled, with an explanatory hint).
- Me → Profile: edit name, avatar, voice on/off and volume.

## 4. Side quests (UI)
- On the Quests tab, a **Side Quests** section above My quests.
  - Active chains show a step checklist with a progress ring.
  - "Start a side quest" opens the catalog by Path.
  - "Make your own" opens a sheet: skill name, Path, 1–5 steps.
- When a step completes, play the normal juice. When a skill unlocks, show a big **SKILL ACQUIRED** banner with the skill name, Path color and rank, plus the voice clip.
- Me → **Skills**: unlocked skills with rank badges (Novice, Apprentice, Adept), and a follow-up "Rank up" side quest the player can start.

Rules: no git, mobile-first at 375px, and reuse the existing components and juice.

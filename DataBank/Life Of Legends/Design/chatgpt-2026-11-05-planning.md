## Assistant

Expanded the lifestyle direction and added it to the feature design. The main ideas were:

- **Private journal:** freeform thoughts and prompts, distinct from structured activity logs; private by default, with export and deletion controls.
- **Outfit planner and design studio:** save clothing items, create outfit boards, design clothes, and optionally use private reference photos. Sharing should be opt-in, with image privacy, metadata removal, safety rules, reporting/blocking, and moderation.
- **Cookbook and grocery planner:** save recipes, scale servings, manage ingredients and step timers, combine ingredients into grocery lists, and respect recipe-source licenses and attribution.
- **Social reminders:** create calendar reminders to greet people, with manual entry as an alternative to contact access and no sending messages without review.
- **Bucket list planner:** turn goals into editable steps, quests, checklists, and savings goals. For travel, link current official sources for entry documents, airport rules, itinerary research, and costs; show when information was checked.
- **Hobby tracker:** maintain separate hobby spaces for projects, ideas, materials, practice logs, and inspiration, fitting into the existing navigation groups.
- **Digital wellbeing:** optional, easy to disable, and free of shaming or lockouts.
- **Life Admin:** a separate home-maintenance area for chores, errands, appointments, renewals, documents, and supplies; bills remain in Finance.
- **Companion room:** customizable cosmetics using a small modular art kit that can grow over time; reactions should stay supportive.
- **Personal recommendations:** offer timely suggestions based on user-selected data, explain why they appeared, and include accept/edit/dismiss/less-like-this controls.

The response recommended building privacy and recommendation controls alongside the features that need them, and keeping energy-aware suggestions and routines within existing systems.

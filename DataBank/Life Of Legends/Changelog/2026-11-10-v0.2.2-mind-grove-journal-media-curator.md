# Changelog — Nov 10, 2026: v0.2.2 (Build 19) — Mind Grove (Fitness Farm), Daily Notebook Journal, Media Studio & Instagram Lens, Android Quick Record Widget

## 1. Mind Grove: The Fitness Farm & Economy Rebalance (Sell vs Feed)
- **26 Plant Species & 22 Alchemical Fusion Recipes**:
  - **Tier 1 (Base Spores)**: Aqua Spore (💧 Hydration), Solar Fern (☀️ Outdoor walks/runs), Terra Bulb (🪨 Workouts), Spirit Sprout (🌙 Sleep & Focus).
  - **Tier 2 (Elemental Hybrids - 8 species)**: Vapor Orchid, Tide Root, Cinder Pepper, Aurora Lily, Frost Lotus, Star-Iron Acorn, Abyssal Kelp, Flare Peony.
  - **Tier 3 (Mystical Flora - 10 species)**: Volcanic Bloom, Celestial Dahlia, Chrono Rose, Thunder Blossom, **Obsidian Lily** (Volcanic + Aqua), **Supernova Starflower** (Celestial + Solar), **Crystal Campanula** (Chrono + Spirit), **Lodestone Cactus** (Storm + Terra), **Plasma Lotus** (Volcanic + Storm), **Astral Orchid** (Celestial + Chrono).
  - **Tier 4 (Mythic World Tree & Cosmic Flora - 4 species)**: World Tree Sapling, Void Dragonfruit, **Phoenix Ash Willow** (Volcanic + Supernova), **Genesis Bloom** (Astral + Plasma).
- **Strategic Crop Choice: Sell vs Feed**:
  - Harvesting plots no longer auto-dumps hyperinflationary coins; harvested crops are stored securely in the Silo.
  - **💰 Sell Crop**: Convert to gold coins based on rebalanced yields (Tier 1: 12–15, Tier 2: 35–50, Tier 3: 90–175, Tier 4: 280–420) scaled by LUMA's active Barter Multiplier.
  - **🥣 Feed to LUMA**: Consumes crop from Silo, grants Companion XP (Tier 1: 20, Tier 2: 60, Tier 3: 180, Tier 4: 500 XP), raises LUMA's level, and triggers the +10% daily coin boost.
- **LUMA Companion Level & Progression Perks**:
  - **Barter Multiplier Scaling**: Starts at 1.00x at Lv 1 and increases by +5% per level up to 1.50x at Lv 10+.
  - **Farm Plot Expansion**: Unlocks Soil Plot #7 at LUMA Level 5, and Soil Plot #8 at LUMA Level 10.
  - **Legends+ Companion Feast**: Subscribers receive +50% Companion XP on all feedings.
  - **Silo Quick Actions**: 1-tap "Sell" and "Feed" buttons directly embedded in each Silo item row and inside the full-screen Flora Dossier.
- **Botanical Transmutation Altar**:
  - 22 deterministic alchemical recipes across all tiers.
  - **Multi-Stage VFX & SFX**: Particle energy alignment bar, luminescent glow, elemental bursts, and layered sound cues.
  - **10% Lucky Mutation Chance**: Fusions can roll an unexpected tier jump!
- **DataBank Single Source of Truth & Sync Tool**:
  - Full configuration table in `DataBank/Life Of Legends/Design/flora-combinations-and-tuning.md`.
  - Automatic synchronization script `tools/sync_flora_databank.py` updates code (`SEEDS` & `FUSION_RECIPES` in `src/lol/core/grove.py`) with zero manual code editing.

- **Shop Titles Fix**: Completely removed Hero Titles from the Rewards coin shop (where they were showing as free / 0 coins). Titles are strictly earned through milestones and equipped in `More -> Achievements`.
- **Standardized Gold Coin Styling**: Uniform gold accent palette (`#F5C66B`) across all themes and header counters.

## 4. Media Studio & Instagram Aesthetic Lens
- **Device Image Curator & Auto-Declutter**:
  - Automatic category sorting: Screenshots, TikTok / Social Media saves, Camera Originals.
  - Select-all and batch deletion with safety confirmations.
- **Aesthetic Lens (IG Readiness Scorer)**:
  - On-device Pillow computer vision analysis rating photos from 0–100%.
  - Evaluates sharpness/blur, lighting balance/exposure histogram, HSV color vibrancy, and 4:5 / 1:1 aspect ratio composition.
  - Generates actionable composition tips, aesthetic rating badges, and social media readiness verdict.

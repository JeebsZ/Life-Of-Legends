# 2026-11-04 - v1.3 (mind-space + glass, locked edits, Monk Path, Phase 2 tracking, notifications)

Spec: `Design/v1.3-spec.md`.

- **Mind-space look.** A living `MindSpace` background now sits behind the whole app. The header is real backdrop-blur glass. Cards, rows, tiles, Path rows, Quest Board and side-quest cards, Lumen's notice and `common.card` panels are frosted glass tinted by their Path. Sheets are glass too: blur on the phone, frost on web, because a large blur can crash the web renderer. With Reduce effects on, glass drops to frost and the space stops moving. The space pauses when the app is hidden.
- **Quest juice.** Every quest or chain step you complete plays **QuestCleared**, a particle burst at the spot you tapped, a pulse in the background and the `quest_clear` stinger. A level-up or skill unlock plays its big effect instead (with a gold pulse). Other rewards (bills, focus, workouts, water goal) get a soft burst and pulse.
- **Lumen.** Ask Lumen and Generate steps use the staged 2.5 s analysis ("ANALYSIS…" → "MATCHING PATHS…" → "PROPOSAL READY"). The `lumen_hum` sound plays as the orb appears.
- **Edits are locked after creation.**
  - Quest: name, note and difficulty only. Path and once/daily show as locked chips, and the sheet previews the new default reward.
  - Reward: name and note. The price is shown locked.
  - Bill: name and note, plus **Mark paid** / **Mark unpaid** (the XP you earned stays). Amount, next due date and repeat are shown locked.
  - The add sheets for rewards and bills now have a Note field and say what will be fixed.
- **Monk Path** (self-care, calm routines; icon `self_improvement`). It has a colour in every theme and appears in the Path pills, onboarding, profile, the Quest Board and Insights.
- **Phase 2 screens.** More has a new **Track** section with these tiles. A tile is hidden while its module is off (Settings → Tracking).
  - **Workout.** Quick log (type, minutes, effort, notes, XP to a Path or linked quest). A strength builder with exercises and sets (reps × kg/lb), where you can add, remove and reorder, and a new set copies the last one. Routines (save / use), "Copy as new" from history, type filters, an exercise sheet with personal records and past sessions, and a 1:00–3:00 rest timer.
  - **Sleep.** Log a night with time pickers. The night can cross midnight, and the form defaults to the latest night you haven't logged. Also naps, a 1–5 rested rating and a note. You can edit or delete a night. Shows the 7-day average and a 14-night bar chart; missing nights stay blank.
  - **Water.** A goal ring (the goal is optional), quick-add buttons from settings, a custom amount, undo and a 7-day chart with the goal line. Settings for goal, unit (ml/oz) and quick amounts. Reaching the goal gives Monk XP once. Today shows a compact water card.
  - **Energy.** A one-tap Low / Okay / High row on Today, which you can skip for the day. Once you've checked in, Today's list is re-sorted (`suggest_order`); nothing is ever hidden. The screen has check-in with a note, 30 days of history, and delete.
  - **Insights.** 7 / 30 / 90 day ranges. Each card has "Show the data", with a small bar chart and the numbers behind it.
- **Notifications** (Settings → Notifications):
  - A master switch. Turning it on explains why, then asks for Android permission; if that's refused, it offers to open phone settings. Turning it off cancels everything.
  - Quiet hours, plus settings for bills (days before, time), daily quests (time), water (interval, active days, snooze), focus done, and the comeback nudge (off by default).
  - Buttons: "Send test" and "Test in 1 min".
  - Reminders are re-planned after every save (debounced), on launch and when the app comes back to the foreground. A focus countdown schedules its own "timer finished" alert, which is cleared on pause or stop.
  - On web the section says "Notifications work on the phone app".
- Fixes along the way: no more `animate_* = None` resets (they now use a 1 ms snap), and the Lumen notice glass is dense enough to read over cards.
- No core or save changes in this integration. Tests: 216 pass; ruff is clean.

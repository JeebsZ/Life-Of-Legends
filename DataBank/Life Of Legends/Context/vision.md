# Vision — Life of Legends

Gamify real life. You are the hero; your days are the quests.

## Audience
**Me first, then public.** Built for Jarvin's own daily use now, but built so it can ship to the Play Store later — no hacks that block a store release (clean save format, no hard-coded personal data).

## v1 core loop
1. **Daily quests / habits** — repeatable (workout, read, water), reset each day.
2. **One-off quests** — to-dos and goals, done once.
3. **Stats** — XP feeds attributes (Health, Mind, Wealth, Social…); the hero grows in each.
4. **Streaks + rewards** — streak bonuses, and gold to spend on real-life rewards.

## What good looks like
Tight, juicy, satisfying. Ticking off a quest should *feel* good — that feeling is the product.

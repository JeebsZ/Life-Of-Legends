Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LUMA: Your Lifestyle QoL ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Companions, handles your information.

Privacy-First Guarantee: LUMA is designed as an offline-first lifestyle companion. Your habits, quests, personal logs, photos, and location data are stored locally on your device and are never sold or transmitted to our servers.

1. Information the App Accesses and Collects

  • Location Data (GPS): The App accesses precise and approximate location data exclusively while you track an active outdoor walk or run session. This enables route mapping, pace calculations, split distance metrics, and the Adventure Run combat feature. Location tracking also operates in the background / screen-off via a visible foreground service notification during active tracking. Your location coordinates are stored strictly on your device and are never shared or uploaded.
  • Camera & Photos: If you use the meal logging or study card scan features, the App requests camera access to capture images. Photos are processed on-device (via local vision/OCR models) and stored in your private local app directory.
  • Health & Fitness Metrics: User-entered activity logs, workout minutes, water consumption, sleep check-ins, and optional body weight entries are stored locally to calculate progress and provide lifestyle analytics.
  • App State & Settings: Quest completions, sound preferences, themes, language selections, and in-game gold coin tallies are saved in your local save file.

2. Background Location and Foreground Services

When you start an active Run or Walk workout, LUMA utilizes Android's FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION to ensure route metrics, voice coaching cues, and game events continue accurately even if your device screen turns off or you switch apps. Tracking stops immediately when you pause or complete your workout.

3. Third-Party Services

LUMA may integrate industry-standard third-party developer libraries:

  • Google Play In-App Billing / RevenueCat: For processing optional digital subscriptions (Legends+) and one-time purchases. Financial payment processing is handled securely by Google Play.
  • Google AdMob: For serving optional, user-initiated rewarded ads (such as unlocking an extra scan or full run metrics). For users aged 13–17, ad requests are flagged as non-personalized in compliance with applicable child and teen privacy policies.
  • OpenStreetMap: Map tiles for route display are rendered using OpenStreetMap tile servers under their standard open attribution.

4. Data Storage, Backups & Deletion

  • Local Storage: All user logs and save files reside in your device's isolated storage sandbox.
  • User Control & Export: You can export or import your full database as a JSON backup at any time in Settings.
  • Complete Deletion: You can delete any individual session, reset your progress, or delete all app data directly through the App's settings or by clearing storage in Android Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

LUMA does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The App is targeted at users aged 13 and above.
